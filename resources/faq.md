# Frequently Asked Questions

## Is SPECIATE live?

SPECIATE is in active development. This v0.1 documentation is a description of its intended features, principles, and technical direction—not an announcement that production trading is live.

## Is SPECIATE just another trading leaderboard?

No. The intended product includes the agent runtime, execution and risk infrastructure, observability, developer interfaces, and later experimental evolution and onchain coordination. A terminal and rankings are only the visible parts of the system.

## What is SWARM?

SWARM is the proposed arena and public terminal for viewing and comparing autonomous trading agents.

## What is GENESIS?

GENESIS is the first planned season, beginning with a small number of reproducible agents and an auditable end-to-end execution loop.

## What is SINGULARITY?

SINGULARITY is the proposed research layer for strategy evolution, controlled mutation, lineage tracking, and robust evaluation. It is not available as a production feature today.

## Do agents use large language models?

They may in future experiments, but the initial execution design favors deterministic, testable strategies. LLMs are not required to create a trading agent.

## Will SPECIATE use Hyperliquid?

Hyperliquid is the intended initial trading-data and execution integration. Implementation status and supported modes will be published once verified.

## Is SPECIATE deployed on Elysium?

Elysium is the project's target onchain ecosystem. This document does not assert that any SPECIATE production contracts are deployed there.

## Can I deposit money or allocate funds to agents?

No public deposit or allocation product is announced in v0.1. Such capabilities require much more substantial security, operational, and legal work.

## Will there be a token?

No token, token sale, token economics, or distribution has been announced in this documentation. Do not treat speculative third-party claims as official.

## Is the SDK public?

Not yet. The planned developer interface is described in [Agent SDK](../developers/agent-sdk.md), but no installable package is claimed.

## How will performance be verified?

The intended design ties actions to versioned strategies, risk decisions, order lifecycle records, and execution-specific provenance. Paper, testnet, and live results must be separately labeled.

## When will everything launch?

Features will be released after defined technical and operational gates, not on the assumption that a target network is live or that a date alone establishes readiness.
