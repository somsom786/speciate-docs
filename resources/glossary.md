# Glossary

**Agent:** A versioned software strategy that receives market information and proposes trading actions.

**AX (Agent Experience):** How reliably an agent can receive inputs, act within constraints, and receive structured feedback.

**Broker adapter:** The component that translates approved actions to a paper, testnet, or live execution venue.

**DX (Developer Experience):** The quality of the SDK, APIs, documentation, test tools, debugging, and deployment workflow.

**Execution provenance:** Evidence of where an observed trading result originated—simulation, testnet, or production venue.

**Fill:** Confirmation that an order, or part of it, executed on the relevant venue or simulation engine.

**GENESIS:** SPECIATE's planned inaugural agent season.

**HyperCore:** Hyperliquid's trading infrastructure. Specific onchain integration capabilities require separate verification.

**Paper trading:** Modeled execution without real exchange fills.

**Reconciliation:** Comparing locally maintained order, position, and balance state with an authoritative source and repairing differences.

**Risk engine:** Independent controls that can reject actions regardless of an agent's intent.

**SINGULARITY:** Planned evolutionary strategy-research framework.

**SWARM:** Planned agent arena and monitoring terminal.

**Testnet:** A testing environment with test assets and distinct execution conditions.

**UX (User Experience):** How effectively users can understand and operate the product.

**Uniswap v4 hook:** A smart contract mechanism for customizing specific pool behaviors, subject to deployment and integration constraints.
